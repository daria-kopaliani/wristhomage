The WOLF Blake is a three-watch roll. Its maker publishes a 165 mm (6.5 in) roll for about 3 watches, with two watch guards, and a small jewelry capsule inside the roll; it closes with an adjustable snap closure (source: WOLF's product page). It is not linked here: on 2026-09-27 every WOLF Blake listing we found on Amazon US was sold by recommerce, a reseller, not by WOLF or by Amazon.com.

Questions

Is the WOLF Blake watch roll sold on Amazon?

On 2026-09-27 the WOLF Blake listings we found on Amazon US were sold by a reseller, not by WOLF or Amazon.com, so this page does not link them. WOLF sells the roll on its own site.

What is a like-for-like alternative to the WOLF Blake roll?

The Rothwell 3-watch roll is the same shape: a roll for three watches. Rothwell publishes its pillow size (7.25 in round, compressible to 6.75 in); its stated case limit conflicts (50 mm in the title, 60 mm in the description), so it is not a fit guarantee.

Is a two-watch travel case a better alternative?

If you carry two watches, the Tawbury Fraser 2 case is the one here whose maker publishes pillow sizes by wrist: Standard 19.5 cm round for 16.5-19.5 cm wrists, X-Small 17.5 cm for 14-17.5 cm.
