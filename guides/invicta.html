What Invicta is

Invicta says in its own store copy that it was founded in 1837. It sells dozens of lines, but the Pro Diver is the one that matters here: a coin-edge bezel, a date window and a steel bracelet. It is the Submariner shape at an entry price. Unlike most houses on this site, Invicta is sold by Amazon.com itself: all four watches below were sold and shipped by Amazon.com on the day we checked.

The movements, calibre by calibre

Invicta's store prints a spec table for every reference, and it names the calibre and its vendor:

  • NH35A automatic, 24 jewels, vendor code TMI: the 40mm Pro Divers 8926OB, 8927OB, 8928OB and 8929OB, and the 43mm 9403OBXL. The NH35A is Seiko's own automatic calibre. It hacks, it hand-winds, and it is the same engine most budget homages on this site use.
  • PC32A quartz, vendor code TMI: the 37.5mm Pro Divers 8932OB and 9204OB. They look almost identical in photos, so read the reference before you read anything else.

Every other Pro Diver in that table lists a mineral crystal. The 8926OB's spec table has no crystal field, and its Amazon listing names a scratch-resistant mineral crystal. The tables also list a screw-down crown on the automatics (a push crown on the quartz) and 200m of water resistance.

Where it falls short

Three things. The crystal is mineral, not sapphire, so it scratches sooner: Invicta's spec tables say so for the siblings, and the 8926OB's Amazon listing says so for it. The case is thick for a 40mm diver, at 14.1mm on the 8926OB listing. And the bezel and bracelet are a clear step below San Martin or Steeldive finishing. Its fidelity score of 80 on our rubric reflects the design, and the rubric does not score build quality.

Verdict: the Invicta 8926OB is still the simplest way to a Submariner-shaped automatic: a real Seiko NH35A, 200m, sold by Amazon.com, with no warehouse lottery. Buy the 40mm automatic, check the reference ends in the right number, and accept mineral glass. For sapphire and a ceramic bezel, see Steeldive or Addiesdive.

FAQ

Are Invicta watches any good?
The automatic Pro Divers are, for what they are. The 8926OB and its colour siblings put a Seiko-made NH35A automatic in a 40mm steel case with a screw-down crown, a unidirectional bezel and a 200m rating, according to Invicta's own spec tables. The compromises are a mineral crystal rather than sapphire (the 8926OB's Amazon listing names a scratch-resistant mineral crystal), and a coin-edge case that wears thicker than the Submariner it follows: the 8926OB's Amazon listing gives 14.1mm.

What movement is in the Invicta 8926OB?
Invicta's own store lists the 8926OB's movement calibre as NH35A, 24 jewels, from the vendor it codes TMI. The NH35A is Seiko's automatic workhorse, with hacking seconds and hand-winding. The 8926OB's Amazon listing says the same and adds that the watch is assembled in Malaysia.

Which Invicta Pro Diver is quartz?
The 37.5mm references. Invicta's store lists the 8932OB and the 9204OB with a PC32A quartz calibre, and the 40mm 8926OB, 8927OB, 8928OB and 8929OB with the NH35A automatic. The reference number tells you which one you are buying, and the listing photos do not.

Is Invicta a homage brand?
The Pro Diver coin-edge line is Invicta's Submariner-style watch, and our Submariner page ranks the 8926OB at fidelity 80. Invicta sells many other lines that follow no single original. This guide covers only the Pro Divers.
