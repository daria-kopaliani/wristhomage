What Sugess is

Sugess is a Chinese brand selling direct from sugesswatch.com (397 products at our 28 September 2026 read) and through its own Amazon storefront, Sugess Official. Where most budget homage houses fit a Seiko NH35 and stop there, Sugess sells the movements Seagull is known for. Its store tags some products with the Rolex reference they follow, such as 116610LN, 126710BLRO and Daytona. Most of its catalogue follows no single original.

The movements, calibre by calibre

  • Seagull ST19: hand-wound column-wheel chronograph, 21,600 bph, over 35 hours of reserve on the S465 page. It is in the S465 and the SUM199 moonphase chronograph.
  • Seagull ST1721: automatic jump hour with a single pointer, in the S451 Art Master.
  • Seagull ST2528: automatic moonphase with date, 21,600 bph, over 48 hours on the SU2528 page.
  • Other lines use other movements. The Ocean Star divers list a Seiko NH35, the Heritage S463 GMT a Peacock SL3032, and our S466 row a Miyota 9015.

Where it falls short

Water resistance and fulfilment. Most Sugess watches are rated 50m, so they are not swimming watches, however sporty the dial looks. The Amazon listings are merchant-fulfilled by Sugess Official, marked “usually ships within 2 to 3 days”, so delivery is slower than a Prime item. The S465 is a vintage-chronograph shape rather than a line-for-line Daytona, and our rubric scores it that way.

Verdict: Sugess is the budget house for mechanical complications rather than dive specs. It has a real column-wheel chronograph, a jump hour and a true moonphase, each with its Seagull calibre named by the maker. Buy one for the movement, and keep it away from the pool. For a dive-rated homage, see Steeldive or Heimdallr.

FAQ

Are Sugess watches any good?
Yes, if you want a mechanical complication cheaply. Sugess builds around Seagull movements, including a hand-wound column-wheel chronograph (the ST19), an automatic jump hour (ST1721) and an automatic moonphase (ST2528). Its own store lists sapphire on the chronographs and moonphases and 50m of water resistance on most. The catch is size and water resistance: these are 38–41mm dress-leaning watches, not divers.

What movement does Sugess use?
Mostly Tianjin Seagull calibres, named on each product page: the ST19 manual-wind column-wheel chronograph (S465 and the SUM199 moonphase chronograph), the ST1721 automatic jump hour (S451) and the ST2528 automatic moonphase (SU2528). Some lines use other movements. The Ocean Star divers list a Seiko NH35, and the Heritage S463 GMT lists a Peacock SL3032.

Where can you buy Sugess watches?
