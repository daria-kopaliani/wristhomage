The Explorer is the easiest icon to get wrong, because almost everything about it is restraint — a black dial, 3-6-9 numerals, no date, no bezel. There is nothing to copy except proportion, which is exactly what most homages miss by wearing 39 or 40mm. Five watches in our database, ranked on our fidelity rubric. Every fidelity score here follows the published rubric, and each model number and price is read from the brand’s own store, with the date recorded against the row.

Stock reality, checked 28 August 2026: the two highest-fidelity entries here are both hard to buy. The San Martin SN021-G was sold out in every variant on San Martin's own store, and the Pagani PD-1751 was sold out on Pagani's. The Addiesdive pair and the Baltany are the live ones, and the AD2556 is what we would actually buy today — re-checked on Addiesdive's own store 30 August, listed at $149.99 and in stock.

Why 36mm is the whole argument

The modern Explorer (ref. 124270) is 36mm. That is small by current taste and it is the point — the watch reads as a tool watch precisely because it does not fill a wrist. A 40mm black-dial three-hander with 3-6-9 numerals is not an Explorer homage; it is a field watch that happens to share a dial layout.

This is the one category where the cheap watches often get the important thing right and the expensive ones do not. Every entry below is 36mm.

1. Addiesdive AD2556 — the best you can actually buy Amazon ↗

About $150, fidelity 82. A genuine ST2130 automatic — a Swiss-architecture movement rather than a Seiko or Miyota — under a sapphire bubble crystal, at the correct 36mm. In stock on Addiesdive's own store at our 30 August check, which is more than the two highest-scoring entries here can say. The bubble crystal is a vintage flourish the modern Explorer does not have; that is the main place it departs.

FAQ

What is the best Rolex Explorer homage?
The Addiesdive AD2556 at about $150 is the best buyable one — ST2130 automatic, sapphire, 36mm. The San Martin SN021-G scores higher but is sold out.

Why does 36mm matter?
The Explorer is a 36mm watch. At 39 or 40mm it stops reading as an Explorer and becomes a generic black-dial field watch.

Is there a cheap one?
The AD2515 at about $79 — right size and dial, but VH31 quartz rather than automatic.
